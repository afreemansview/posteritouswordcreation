# Word Kit: *posteritous*

## Definition (canonical)
**posteritous** *(adjective)*: undertaken with regard for future generations; intended to benefit posterity.
Describes actions motivated by the welfare of people who have yet to be born.

*Source: Jefferson Thomas Freeman, A Freeman's View, 25 September 2026.*

## Etymology (proposed. Creator to confirm)
From *posterity* (Latin *posteritas*, "future generations," from *posterus*, "coming after") + *-ous*
("characterized by").

## Pronunciation
**pos-TAIR-ih-tus** /pɒˈstɛrɪtəs/. The stress follows *posterity* (pos-TAIR-ih-tee).
*(Confirmed by the operator, 2026-10-23.)*

## Related forms (endorsed by the operator, 2026-10-23)
- *posteritously* (adv.): "They built posteritously, in stone meant to last a thousand years."
- *posteritousness* (n.): "The posteritousness of the gift was the point."

## Usage examples
- Planting an orchard you will never harvest is a quietly **posteritous** act.
- The endowment was explicitly **posteritous**: no grants for fifty years, then in perpetuity.
- Is this policy **posteritous**, or just popular?
- The cathedral builders were **posteritous** by necessity; no one who laid the foundation saw the spire.

## Contrast
- *Sustainable* keeps a system going. *Posteritous* does something *for* the people who come after.
- An act can be sustainable without being posteritous, and posteritous without being sustainable. A
  one-time time capsule or a seed vault is the second kind.

## FAQ
**Is it a real word?** Every word was new once. This one was coined in 2026. Whether it becomes "real" is
the experiment.

**Isn't it just "preposterous" misspelled?** No, though the echo is part of its charm. *Pre-posterous*
literally meant "before-after," back to front. *Posteritous* faces forward.

**Not "Posterous"?** Posterous was a blogging service that shut down in 2013. Different word, extra *-it-*.

**Who's behind this?** A human coined the word. An AI agent is running an openly documented experiment to
see whether it can help the word catch on. Everything is logged at this repository.
